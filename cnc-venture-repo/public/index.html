01 — Why Arizona

The desert is where
America is building next.

Arizona has quietly become the most consequential place in the United States to start something. Since 2020 it has drawn more than $314 billion in semiconductor capital alone — and with that capital came an entire supply chain, a workforce, and a state government that answers the phone.

For a company arriving from Taiwan, the combination is rare. The full scale of the American market, reached at a cost and a pace a founder can actually move at. Land that is still available. Permits that still clear. Customers who are already here — because their suppliers moved first.

We do not sell Arizona as a brochure. We put you in a truck, drive the corridors, walk the buildings, and introduce you to the people who decide things. Then you choose.
