01 — Why Arizona

The desert is where
America is building next.

Arizona has emerged as one of the most dynamic gateways for companies entering the U.S. market. Its growing semiconductor and advanced manufacturing ecosystem, business-friendly environment, expanding population, and strong public-private partnerships create meaningful opportunities for international businesses.

For companies arriving from Taiwan, Arizona offers access to a growing market, an established Taiwanese business community, and local partners who understand the realities of cross-border expansion.

CnC Venture helps companies evaluate these opportunities firsthand through market research, site visits, government and industry introductions, professional advisory resources, and practical local execution.

06 — Arizona City Guide

One valley.
Six very different answers.

Greater Phoenix is not a single market. Each city serves different customers, industries, and business models — and the right address depends on which of them you need.

Phoenix

The centre of gravity
Government, business services, major commercial districts, and access to the broader metro market.

Scottsdale

Where the market pays a premium
Premium hospitality, dining, tourism, lifestyle retail, and affluent customers.

Tempe

The talent
ASU, startups, innovation, young professionals, and technology-oriented businesses.

Mesa

Room to build
Industrial growth, logistics, aerospace, land availability, and expanding residential communities.

Gilbert

The family market
Family-oriented communities, strong household demographics, retail, and dining opportunities.

Chandler

The technology corridor
Semiconductors, technology, advanced manufacturing, corporate offices, and international supply chains.
